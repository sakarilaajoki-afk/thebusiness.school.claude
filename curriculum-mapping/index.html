What the reports contain

At the end of the lesson each student downloads a personal report of their recorded decisions and question results. The teacher downloads the class report: each firm's result, each student's recorded actions and questions, and each final question with its topic and how many students answered it correctly. These records support follow-up discussion. They are indicative, not official or predicted exam results, and simulation records do not count as BTEC qualification assessment evidence.

How to read it

This guide to topics was prepared by TBS Education with reference to the published Pearson specifications (GCSE 1BS0 Issue 2; A Level 9BS0 Issue 1; BTEC 601/7159/5), checked on 8 November 2026. A recorded action shows that a decision was taken in the simulation, not that the topic is understood: use it as a prompt for questioning, not as a judgement. Team chat is never treated as curriculum evidence.

Your qualification and level

Before the lesson, choose your class. The list has 14 exam board courses and 7 plain classes, such as GCSE Business. Your choice sets the level (Level 2, GCSE standard, or Level 3, A level standard) and the topics on offer, and names your class in the reports.

Six final questions on the lesson you picked, written at GCSE or A level standard, not for one specification. Each question has its own topic, shown on the class report. The questions are not endorsed by any awarding body.
