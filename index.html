In one lesson

How to use it

1

Get ready

Log in with the teacher password and your licence key. Choose the course, the year group and how long the simulation runs, from 30 to 75 minutes. You get a 6-digit PIN.

2

Students join

Students open play.thebusiness.school on a laptop, desktop or Chromebook and type the PIN and a first name. When everyone is in, open firm setup: the game makes firms of 3 or 4 and gives everyone a role.

3

Run the lesson

Three rounds run on the clock. Each student’s Do now card shows their next step. Pause to discuss, and start the round 3 crisis when it suits you.

4

After the lesson

Each student writes a short reflection and downloads their own report. Your class analysis arrives by email within 48 hours.

After every session

Every student gets a personal report

When the simulation ends, each student downloads a private report of what they did, not a generic score. It is indicative, not an exam result, and it ends with a certificate of completion that records participation only. Assessment stays with the teacher.

Technical details

Built for the classroom, no IT department required

Browser-based

Runs in a modern browser on laptops, desktops and Chromebooks, one screen per student. No downloads, plugins or installations.

Zero student accounts

Students join with a PIN and a first name or nickname. No email addresses, no passwords.

Quick setup

The 6-digit PIN appears as soon as you create the session. Once the class has joined, firm setup takes up to 3 minutes, then round 1 starts.

School Wi-Fi compatible

Lightweight data usage, compatible with filtered school networks.

Data stored in the EU

Session data is held server-side in an EU database in Frankfurt, and a full class of 32 plays at the same time.

Questions

What teachers ask first

Is it free for teachers?＋

The teacher demo is free and needs no credit card. Licensing is per school (or per MAT), per year, with a free classroom pilot before any commitment.

Which levels does it fit?＋

The game runs at two levels: Level 2 (GCSE-level) and Level 3 (A level-level). When you set up a lesson you choose your qualification; that sets the level and the topics on offer, and names your course in the reports. The Knowledge Check questions are written for the level and the topic, not for one specification. During play students see plain instructions, not specification codes.

Do students need to install anything or make accounts?＋

No. It runs in the browser on a laptop, desktop or Chromebook, one screen per student. Students open the link and join with a 6-digit PIN and a first name, nothing to download, no logins to manage.

How many students, and how long does it take?＋

From 6 to 32 students, in up to 8 firms of 3 or 4. After up to 3 minutes of firm setup there are three rounds, and you set each round to 10, 15, 20 or 25 minutes. The rounds move on by themselves when the clock runs out, so it never stalls.

What does the teacher see and control?＋

A live control panel: pause and resume the rounds, start the round 3 crisis, see every student’s role and activity, and project the live chart of every firm’s net position. Afterwards each student downloads a private report, and your class analysis arrives by email within 48 hours. The game checks only the multiple-choice Knowledge Check answers. Nothing is turned into a mark, and assessment stays with the teacher.
