Our story

Built from a real lesson no classroom ever taught

That one mistake led to €8,400 in debt recovery. No teacher, no textbook and no careers advisor had ever mentioned it could happen.

A growing share of students already run a side hustle by sixth form. Social media has made entrepreneurship feel easy, but nobody teaches the financial fundamentals that make or break a young business.

What if students could make those mistakes safely, before they cost real money? That question became The Business School.

“At 17, I organised large-scale events, took real financial risks, and learned the hard way what happens when you miss a tax return.”

Founder, TBS Education Ltd Oy · Lahti, Finland

Proudly built in Finland, where pedagogy is founded on trust, equality and learning by doing.

Before this game, I ran a shop and a live event in Lahti

I organised a live music event and ran a clothing shop, Suitman, with a business partner. The local paper wrote about both. The shop closed in January 2026. Selling branded clothing in that city was not very profitable. I learned that by running it, not from a lesson. That is why students in The Business School run a firm and make the decisions themselves.

Technical details

Built for the classroom, no IT department required

Browser-based

Runs in a modern browser on laptops, desktops, Chromebooks and iPads, one screen per student. No downloads, plugins or installations.

Zero student accounts

Students join with a PIN and a first name or nickname. No email addresses, no passwords.

Quick setup

Questions

What teachers ask first

Is it free for teachers?＋

The teacher demo is free and needs no credit card. Licensing is per school (or per MAT), per year, with a free trial lesson before any commitment.

Which levels does it fit?＋

The game runs at two levels: Level 2 (GCSE-level) and Level 3 (A level-level). When you set up a lesson you choose your qualification; that sets the level and the topics on offer, and names your class in the reports. The final questions are written for the level, not for one specification. During play students see plain instructions, not specification codes.

Do students need to install anything or make accounts?＋

No. It runs in the browser on a laptop, desktop, Chromebook or iPad, one screen per student. Students open the link and join with a 6-digit PIN and a first name, nothing to download, no logins to manage.

How many students, and how long does it take?＋

Up to 40 students. Most classes play in teams of 3 or 4, and smaller classes run a firm each. You choose a lesson of 30, 45, 60 or 75 minutes: a short firm setup, then three rounds. Each step moves on when every firm is done or the clock runs out, so it never stalls.

What does the teacher see and control?＋

A live control panel: pause and resume the rounds, start an optional class event, see every student’s role and activity, and project the live chart of every firm’s net position. Afterwards each student downloads a private report, and you download the class report. The game checks the topic task answers and the final questions. Nothing is turned into a mark, and assessment stays with the teacher.
