AQA 7138 SMART Objectives: Accountable, Not Achievable

In AQA 7138 the A stands for Accountable, not Achievable. One page that explains it with the specification quoted, and a three objective exercise with full answers for the teacher.

A Level Business  ·  AQA 7137 and 7138  ·  Specification section 3.1.1  ·  Free PDF  ·  No sign up
On AQA 7138 the A is Accountable.

The specification lists the characteristics of effective objectives as Specific, Measurable, Accountable, Realistic, Time specific. Most textbooks and most of the internet use Achievable or Attainable. Those versions are widely used and they are not errors, but they are a different version of the mnemonic, and an answer written for AQA 7138 should use Accountable.

How this one was checked

The one exam board claim in this sheet, the five characteristics and their order, was read out of AQA’s own specification for 7137 and 7138, version 1.1, November 2025, section 3.1.1. That reference is printed on the page beside the quotation, so you can open the PDF and land on it. The one thing that is ours, reading Accountable as a named owner, is labelled on the page as ours and not as an AQA definition.
