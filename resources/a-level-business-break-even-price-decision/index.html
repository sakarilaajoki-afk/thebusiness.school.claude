A-Level Business: Break-even and the Price Decision

Students read a break-even chart, then compare a price cut with extra advertising for the same fictional business and decide which plan to back.

What students do

  • Calculate contribution, break-even output, profit and margin of safety for a candle business as it is today
  • Read the break-even point and the profit at two levels of sales from a chart
  • Complete a table for two separate plans: cut the price to £10, or keep £12 and spend £600 more a month on advertising
  • Test each plan’s sales forecast, then write a recommendation of about 100 words that names one limitation of break-even analysis

What the teacher gets

  • A lesson plan for about 55 minutes of tasks, with a shorter 45-minute version. Allow longer in the timetable for handing out and feedback
  • Worked answers for every question, tolerances for reading the chart and a list of common errors
  • Indicative guidance for the written decision. It is not an exam board mark scheme and carries no marks or grades

Specification

Edexcel A level Business 9BS0, Theme 2, section 2.2.3 Break-even: contribution, break-even point, margin of safety, break-even charts and their limitations. Checked against the Pearson specification, Issue 1, September 2014. The business and every figure are fictional and the questions are original. This is not a Pearson resource, and the lesson has not yet been tested in a classroom.
