What students do

  • Match procurement, just in time and just in case to their meanings
  • Calculate how much coffee a cafe uses, the weekly cost and the cost per cup from three suppliers
  • Work out each supplier’s on-time percentage and what late deliveries would mean
  • Weigh just in time against just in case, rank price, quality and reliability in pairs and recommend one supplier

What the teacher gets

  • A lesson plan for about 55 minutes of tasks, with a shorter 45-minute version. Allow longer in the timetable for handing out and feedback
  • Worked answers for every calculation and question

  • Editable Word versions of both files, so you can change the business or the figures
