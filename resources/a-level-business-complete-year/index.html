Written to be honest about what it is

It is the Year 12 half of the A-level, not both years. Edexcel Themes 3 and 4 and AQA sections 3.7 to 3.10 are the second year and are not in this pack. The page says so, the cover says so, and the pack itself says so on page one.

Every figure is computed, not typed. The businesses in the tasks are written for teaching, the way exam board case material is, and the answers are calculated from the case data so they always reconcile.

Specification references are current. Written for AQA 7132 and Edexcel 9BS0 as they stand. Pearson has confirmed continuity of 9BS0 for 2026 starters. Always check your board's current specification and recent past papers for assessment detail.
