How it fits GCSE / IGCSE teaching

Concrete, not abstract

Price too high and customers leave; overspend and cash runs out. Core concepts land because students feel them.

Whole-class, team-based

Up to 40 students in up to 10 firms, every firm with a CEO, nobody sits out.

Simple to run

Join by PIN on a laptop, desktop, Chromebook or iPad; three rounds of 10 to 25 minutes each.
