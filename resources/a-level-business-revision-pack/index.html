Five company figures corrected. 13 August 2026.

Every number in this pack was checked against the companies' own published results. Five were wrong. None of them is a marking rule, but all five are figures a student could quote in an exam, so here they are in full:

  • The M&S cyber attack was in April 2025, not 2024, and the £300m is M&S's estimate of the hit to operating profit, not lost revenue. Lost online revenue was nearer £175m.
  • Wetherspoon's operating margin for the 52 weeks to 28 July 2024 was 6.9% before separately disclosed items, not 3.8%, and like-for-like sales rose 5.1%, not 5.6%.
  • The JD Sports figures were not the 2024/25 ones. Revenue was £11,458m and cost of sales £5,995m, a gross margin of 47.7%, not 50%.
  • Tesla announced a cut of more than 10% of global headcount in April 2024, about 14,000 jobs. It was not a 14% cut, and 14% of 14,000 jobs never added up.
  • Greggs passed 2,600 UK shops during 2024, not 2025, and the 6% flour saving is an assumption written for the question, not something Greggs reported.

The Word file below is corrected. The PDF is being re-exported and goes back up when it is right, not before. Thank you to anyone who checked a figure rather than scrolling past.

What's inside

Question mix

4 short-answer (4 to 9 marks) · 4 application (10 to 12 marks) · 2 essay/evaluation (16 to 20 marks). Each question is mapped to a specific spec point on the cover.

Mark schemes

Indicative KAAE-structure mark schemes (Knowledge, Application, Analysis, Evaluation) modelled on AQA and Edexcel senior examiner reports.

Examiner tip on every question

Each question ends with the single most common reason students drop marks on that question type, written from the examiner's perspective, not the textbook's.

Bonus: 5 mark-loss patterns

The final page lists the five recurring patterns that cap students at lower levels, share with classes before the next mock.

Real company case studies

Every question uses a real UK or global business, no generic case studies, no AI fluff. Figures are simplified for classroom use and approximate where appropriate.

Greggs JD Sports Boohoo JCB Wetherspoon Shein (London IPO) Costa Coffee × Coca-Cola Tesla M&S cyber-attack Bristol vegan bakery (start-up case)

Spec coverage

AQA 7132: 3.1 What is business · 3.3 to 3.6 Decision making to improve marketing, operational, financial and human resource performance · 3.7 to 3.10 Analysing the strategic position, choosing strategic direction, strategic methods, managing strategic change.

Edexcel 9BS0: Theme 1 Marketing & people · Theme 2 Managing business activities · Theme 3 Business decisions & strategy · Theme 4 Global business.

How to use this pack

  • As mock paper material, pick 5 questions for a 90-minute mock; mark schemes ready.
  • As revision homework, assign one question per night, mark in class the next day using the indicative scheme.
  • As a final-week resource, print the bonus "5 patterns" page and walk students through it before the real exam.
  • Modify and remix, permitted for classroom use. Resell not permitted.
