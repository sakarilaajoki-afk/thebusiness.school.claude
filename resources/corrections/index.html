Corrections

Everything we have got wrong on a resource, when we fixed it, and which resources it touched.

If you downloaded something before 16 August 2026, download it again.

There is no email wall on this site, which means we have no way to reach you when something is corrected. That is the trade. This page is the other half of it: everything that changed is listed here with a date, so you can check the file on your laptop against it in under a minute.

The dated notice inside each pack tells you which version you have. If yours is older than the date below, replace it.

Most of what follows came out of one audit that started on 13 August 2026, after two teachers publicly corrected a free pack of ours within three hours of publication. They were right. The audit that followed read every resource on the site against the boards’ own published documents, and it found more.

Since then every exam board fact lives in a file with the address it came from, and a script checks the whole library against that file before anything goes out. It has already caught errors before publication rather than after, including four in a pack written this week.
