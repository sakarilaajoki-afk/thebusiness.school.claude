Where the marks actually sit on AQA 7136 and Edexcel 9EC0, what each mark scheme actually rewards, and the fifth of the A-level that is arithmetic. Eight pages, every fact from a board document.

A Level Economics  ·  AQA 7136 and Edexcel 9EC0  ·  Year 12, first term  ·  Free PDF and Word  ·  No sign up
AQA’s fifteen mark question rewards no evaluation.

AQA publishes two grids for these papers. The 25 mark grid asks for supported evaluation throughout the response and in a final conclusion. The 15 mark grid never uses the word evaluation at all, at any of its three levels. A student who spends their last four minutes weighing up the argument on a 15 is doing careful work for zero marks, and the marker cannot give it to them.

How this one was checked

Every exam board claim in this pack was read out of that board's own published document, and the section it came from is printed beside it. Page 8 lists the documents so you can settle any of it yourself in about a minute.

That is not a boast, it is a repair. In August 2026 we published a free pack that named the wrong command word for an Edexcel GCSE question, and two teachers caught it publicly within three hours. The fix was to stop writing board facts from memory: every one now lives in a file with the URL it came from, and a script checks the whole library against it before anything goes out.
