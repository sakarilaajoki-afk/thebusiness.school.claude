This pack is being corrected. 13 August 2026.

Two teachers pointed out an error and they were right. The Edexcel 9 mark question is a Justify question with two options, and it does require a judgement. This pack labelled it Analyse and said there was no evaluation credit in it. That is wrong, and following it would have cost students marks.

The download is off while that section is rewritten against the Pearson model answers. It goes back up when it is right, not before.

If you already downloaded it, please do not use the Edexcel 9 mark section. My thanks to the teachers who said something rather than scrolling past.
