Privacy policy

Your data, plainly.

What we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

This policy covers the thebusiness.school website. For how the classroom simulation itself handles data (it is designed so that students never create accounts), see data protection.

What we collect, and why

  • Forms you send us. The waitlist and contact forms collect what you type into them: typically your name, school email, role and school. We use it to reply and to manage the pilot waitlist. Forms are processed by Netlify, our hosting provider.
  • Purchases. Paid products are sold through a checkout provider (Gumroad). They process the payment; we never see your card details. We receive your email address and order details so we can deliver the product and honour the refund policy.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the correspondence so we can help you.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers and no selling of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No student accounts and no student sign ups anywhere on this site.
  • No marketing emails unless you explicitly asked for them.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email support@thebusiness.school and we will act on it. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (we are registered under ICO ZC133810) or to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Where data lives

The website is hosted by Netlify. Form submissions are stored in Netlify's systems. Purchase records are held by the checkout provider. We keep data only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was given.
