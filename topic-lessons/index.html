How a lesson runs

45 minutes, one topic

Here with Break-even as the example.

StartPupils join
You pick the topic and show a PIN. Each pupil joins with a first name and gets their own firm.
Rounds 1 to 3Decide with the numbers
Each round the pupil sets a price and works out the cars to break even. Then they see their result on a chart.
The classCompete
Every pupil's firm is in the same class standing, so the class competes on each round.
Exam practiceTheir own figures
Exam-style questions built from the pupil's own rounds: identify, calculate and explain.
