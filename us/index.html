How a lesson runs

Students learn business by running one.

The game opens with a short guided tour, so students can play without a lecture first. You see every firm live and stop the lesson whenever you want.

1. Buy inventory

Students buy cars at wholesale and watch their cash and free space. A car has to be in inventory before it can be sold.

2. Deal with classmates

Students set prices, make offers and counteroffers, and sell to customers. Nothing is calculated for them: they decide if the price makes a profit.

3. Manage cash

Sales tax is collected on every retail sale and filed each round. Loans carry real interest. Profit and cash are not the same thing.
