The Business School Session end · Net position

Net position decides it.

  • Net position = cash + stock + investments, minus loans and unpaid VAT. Highest at the end wins.
  • Knowledge Check questions appear during play. A correct answer adds £400 to your firm’s cash, a wrong one costs £200.
  • At the end you write a short reflection and download your own report. It records your decisions and answers. It is not an exam mark.
  • Each role has its own jobs on screen, and the whole firm sees the same numbers. Sit together and decide together.
