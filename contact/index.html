Contact

Talk to us.

Support, purchases, pilots or anything else: one address, read by the people who build the product.

Email

support@thebusiness.school

One inbox for everything: support, purchases and refunds, pilot and licensing enquiries, data protection requests, and plain questions. You are writing to the team that builds the product, not a ticket queue.

What to include

  • Support: what you were doing, what happened, and a screenshot if you have one.
  • Purchases and refunds: the email address you bought with and roughly when. See the refund policy.
  • Pilots and licensing: your school, role and rough class sizes. Or use the waitlist form.
  • Data protection: say what you would like us to do with your data. Details in the privacy policy.
