Business Week · 1 to 8 parts
Pilot places for the spring term 2027

Each team’s firm carries on from lesson to lesson.

The class is split into firms, as in the class game. Business Week is played in parts across your normal lessons. Each team’s firm carries on from one part to the next and competes with the other firms. It always ends with a valuation and a winner.

In parts, in your normal lessons

As the firms grow

Crises and big decisions

As the firms grow, they meet crises and have to take big decisions. For example:

Expansion

Each firm decides whether to grow.

Recession

A downturn hits every firm.

A joint bid

A bid together with another firm.

Valuation

At the end, each firm is valued and there is a winner.
