How a task works

A task is a deal

1

A task

A deal or a choice at the firm’s own dealership.

2

One answer

The firm works it out together and agrees one answer.

3

Earn cash

A right answer earns the firm cash.

Six kinds of task

Every topic, in this order

1

A number, or A–D

One answer for the firm.

2

Choose two

Two of five options.

3

Put in order

Three or four steps.

4

Work it out, then choose

Figures first, then A–D.

5

A or B

The answer with its reason.

6

Exam level

A number, or A–D.

Theory names, such as Maslow or Ansoff, appear only if your specification names them.
