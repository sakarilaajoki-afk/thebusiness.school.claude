What The Business School is

The Business School is a live classroom business simulation. Student teams run rival firms in the same market during one lesson: they set prices, hire, take loans, file VAT, face crises and negotiate deals, and every decision has a visible consequence on their firm. Teachers run it from one screen, students join with a PIN, and nothing is installed. Decisions are mapped to the UK exam specifications teachers actually teach.

Around the simulation we publish a growing library of free teaching resources: complete packs with answers, revision pages and classroom games for Business and Economics. The free library stays free. Premium products fund it.

Why it exists

Most business teaching has to happen on paper, and paper cannot answer back. A simulation can. When a student underprices their coffee and watches their cash run out, they have learned something a definition could never teach them. We build so that the first real consequence a young person meets is a safe one, inside a lesson, not an expensive one at 17 with their own money.

Appifest, Sydney

The simulation is built and maintained by Appifest, a development studio in Sydney, Australia, engaged since March 2026 and working to a signed agreement with recorded milestones. The software side does not rest on one person either.

Every line of it belongs to TBS Education Ltd Oy. There is a separate intellectual property assignment covering the source code, the database, the game logic and the documentation. That matters to a school more than the name does: it means the thing you licence does not disappear if a contractor moves on.
