What is inside

One original, fictional UK case for three 55-minute lessons plus named homework; every plan adds up to 55 minutes of task time. A sole-trader mobile dog groomer and her 18-year-old assistant deal with the personal finance sector.

Learners match financial institutions to needs and compare ways of reaching them, decide which regulator or scheme helps in three situations and work out what a complaint or a loan is worth, then use information, guidance and advice sources, compare insurance quotes as a price comparison website would, compare an IVA with bankruptcy in pence in the pound, and recommend a way forward in about 120 to 160 words.

Every rate is invented. It is a teaching case, not financial advice.

  1. Lesson1

    Where could the money go, and how would Nadia reach it?

    Nine types of institution, matching needs to organisations, channels and customers, the time cost of a branch trip, interest at four invented savers.

  2. Lesson2

    When something goes wrong, who protects customers?

    Regulators and schemes from memory, role cards, three situations, complaint value and loan cost, a reply to an 18-year-old.

  3. Lesson3

    Nadia is in debt. Where can she turn?

    Six sources of information and advice, a surplus and months to clear, four insurance quotes compared, IVA against bankruptcy in pence in the pound, a 120 to 160-word recommendation.
