What is inside

One case, 3 lessons, every answer worked.

One original, fictional UK business case for about three 55-minute lessons with Year 13. A family-owned commercial laundry in Lancashire must choose between winning more care homes, a new area, linen rental or wiping cloths.

Students turn a mission statement into objectives and test them against the numbers, use portfolio analysis, Ansoff's Matrix, Porter's Strategic Matrix and distinctive capabilities, build a SWOT, work out what each option needs and earns, then test the options against PESTLE influences, a new rival's price cut and Porter's Five Forces, and write and peer-review a recommendation of about 200 words.

  1. Lesson1

    Where is Harrowfield going?

    Mission and objectives, the numbers behind the objectives, a portfolio analysis grid, Ansoff and Porter matrices, distinctive capabilities, strategic and tactical decisions.

  2. Lesson2

    SWOT and strategic choice

    SWOT and SWOT in numbers, the resources and profit of four options, an objectives test and the strategic choice.

  3. Lesson3

    Outside influences and the recommendation

    PESTLE in pounds, an energy-price rise and the options, Five Forces, a rival's price response, a plan, a 200-word recommendation and peer review.

What this pack is, and is not

  • An original, fictional case. Every business, person, figure and quote is invented; nothing is real data.
  • Timings are estimates. The lessons are not yet classroom-trialled.
  • Written tasks come with indicative guidance and an example answer, not a mark scheme.
  • The Word files were laid out and checked in LibreOffice, not Microsoft Word. Small layout differences are possible when you open them in Word.
  • A classroom resource for teaching and practice. It does not replace a textbook or a scheme of work.
  • The student workbook is 28 pages because every task has full-width ruled space and the case data repeat on the pages that use them.
  • Not endorsed by Pearson. Contains no exam board questions or mark schemes.

Questions teachers ask

What exactly do I get?

A three-lesson student workbook and a teacher guide for one original, fictional case. Both come as PDF and as editable Word files. The teacher guide has the lesson plans, full worked answers, completed diagrams and indicative guidance for the written tasks.

What format are the files?

A4 PDF for printing and projecting, plus the same pages as .docx. The download is one ZIP file that opens on Windows and Mac without extra software.

Can I edit it in Word?

Yes. Change the business name, the figures or the questions before you set it. The Word files were laid out and checked in LibreOffice, not Microsoft Word, so you may see small layout differences when you open them in Word; the PDF is the reference layout.

Is the teacher guide a mark scheme?

No. It gives full answers for the calculations and tables, and indicative guidance plus an example answer for each written task. It is not an exam board mark scheme and the pack awards nothing.

Is it endorsed by the exam board?

No. The pack is mapped to the specification by section, with page references in the teacher guide, but it is not endorsed by Pearson and it contains no exam board questions.

Has it been tried in a classroom?

Not yet. Timings are estimates counted against the written work in each lesson. If you teach it, we would like to hear how the timings held.

How does delivery work?

After payment you are sent straight to your download page and get a Stripe receipt by email. The download page is personal to your purchase; bookmark it.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Email support@thebusiness.school. Our refund policy covers duplicate charges, files you cannot open and products that do not match their description.

What does the licence cover?

One purchase licenses the buying teacher and their school to use the pack with their own classes, including adapting it. Please do not share it publicly, upload it elsewhere or resell it. See the terms.
