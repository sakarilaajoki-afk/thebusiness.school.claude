What is inside

All eight Theme 3 packs in one download: Harrowfield Linen, Marrick Print, Thistledown Garden Centre, Calder Reach Building, Skerrow Buses, Tarnbeck Creamery, Corvane Parcels and Alderscar Furniture. Together they cover Theme 3, business decisions and strategy, from 3.1 to 3.6 with one original, fictional UK business per pack.

Each pack is about three 55-minute lessons with a student workbook and a teacher guide, as PDF and editable Word. Every teacher guide has lesson plans, full worked answers, completed diagrams and indicative guidance for the written tasks.

  1. Pack1

    Harrowfield Linen: where next?

    A family-owned commercial laundry must choose between more care homes, a new area, linen rental or wiping cloths. Objectives, Ansoff, Porter, SWOT, PESTLE and Five Forces. 28-page student workbook, 13-page teacher guide.

  2. Pack2

    Marrick Print: how should we grow?

    A Yorkshire printer can take over a rival, buy a new press, stay small or do nothing. Objectives of growth, market share, economies of scale, overtrading and five-year profit tables. 24-page student workbook, 14-page teacher guide.

  3. Pack3

    Thistledown Garden Centre: forecast first, then invest

    A garden centre forecasts sales with moving averages and a scatter graph, then appraises a café and a glasshouse with payback, ARR and NPV. 20-page student workbook, 13-page teacher guide.

  4. Pack4

    Calder Reach Building: bid, build, on time?

    A small builder decides whether to tender for a sports pavilion, plans the build with a critical path network, then protects the job against a late steel delivery. 20-page student workbook, 12-page teacher guide.

  5. Pack5

    Skerrow Buses: whose interests come first?

    A family-founded bus company weighs old buses against new, evidence against a hunch, culture in two depots, how to share a profit and how far to pay for cleaner buses. 23-page student workbook, 14-page teacher guide.

  6. Pack6

    Tarnbeck Creamery: reading the accounts

    A cheese maker's income statement and statement of financial position, gearing and ROCE, a £300,000 borrowing decision and a recommendation to a bank. 24-page student workbook, 10-page teacher guide.

  7. Pack7

    Corvane Parcels: the people numbers, the causes of change, and which plan?

    A parcel firm with two warehouses is bought by a larger group. Labour productivity, turnover, retention and absenteeism, the causes of change and three HR plans. 24-page student workbook, 12-page teacher guide.

  8. Pack8

    Alderscar Furniture: change, risk and the next move

    A family furniture maker decides how fast to bring in a new computer system, which protections to fund against flood, IT failure and a lost master joiner, and which way to grow. 26-page student workbook, 13-page teacher guide.

Questions

What exactly do I get?

Eight three-lesson student workbooks and eight teacher guides, one pair for each original, fictional case. All come as PDF and as editable Word files, in one ZIP. Each teacher guide has the lesson plans, full worked answers, completed diagrams and indicative guidance for the written tasks.

Can I edit the files?

Yes. The Word files are editable, so you can change the business name, the figures or the questions before you set it. They were laid out and checked in LibreOffice, not Microsoft Word, so you may see small layout differences in Word; the PDF is the reference layout. The download is a ZIP that opens on Windows and Mac without extra software.

Is it a mark scheme, and is it endorsed?

No to both. The teacher guide gives full answers for calculations and tables, and indicative guidance plus an example answer for each written task. It is not an exam board mark scheme, and the packs award no marks or grades. It is mapped to the specification by section but is not endorsed by Pearson, and contains no exam board questions.
