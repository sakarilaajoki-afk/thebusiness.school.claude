What is inside

One original, fictional UK case for about three 55-minute lessons. A family-founded bus company must decide whether to keep or replace its oldest buses (short-termism against long-termism), what its data say about a route the founder wants to scrap (evidence against hunch), how strong the culture is in two depots, how to share a year's profit between shareholders, drivers, passengers and the future, and how far to give up profit for cleaner buses and fairer pay on a school contract.

Students calculate, plot a graph, draw share-of-profit bars, argue for a stakeholder in pairs and write a 180-word recommendation.

  1. Lesson1

    Short term, long term, and how we decide

    Keep-or-replace costs over six years with a graph, Route 14 trip data, four culture types, a depot survey and leaver calculations, a 50-word written task.

  2. Lesson2

    Shareholders and stakeholders

    Stakeholder classification and objectives, three profit-sharing options with dividend and percentage calculations and bars to draw, a pair stakeholder task, 60-word advice.

  3. Lesson3

    Profit, ethics, pay and responsibility

    School contract options with profit, cost per pupil and a tipping price, pay and rewards calculations, CSR costs, a planning page, checklist and 180-word recommendation.
